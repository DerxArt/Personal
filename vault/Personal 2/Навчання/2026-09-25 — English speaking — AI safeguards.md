# English speaking — AI assistant safeguards

- **Format:** live text conversation, about 10 minutes.
- **Scenario:** defend keeping an AI assistant after it canceled a user's meetings.
- **Main idea:** investigate the root cause and learn from failures before abandoning a useful tool. The time saved can go toward family, rest, or a startup.
- **Safeguard proposed:** require approval before actions with serious consequences or actions involving other people. Routine personal activities such as gym time are lower impact.
- **Language focus:** use the base verb after *should*; avoid *will* in the *if*-clause of a first conditional; improve collocations around consequences and planning time.
- **Useful phrases:** root cause analysis; approval gate; high-impact action; serious consequences; learn from failure.
- **Progress signal:** moved from a general defense of the assistant to a concrete approval rule and explained the difference between high- and low-impact actions.
