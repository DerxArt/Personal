# Tutor Otake Page Blueprint

## Page Goal

The page should answer the questions a motivated adult student has before writing in DM:

- Is this tutor relevant for my goal?
- What formats are available?
- How much effort and money will it take?
- Can I book or pay without back-and-forth?
- Does this person look credible?

## Template A - Solo Tutor

Hero:

- Name.
- One-line positioning.
- Photo or clean portrait.
- Primary CTA: Book a lesson.
- Secondary CTA: Ask a question.

Example positioning:

English for professionals who need to speak clearly at work, calls, and interviews.

Core blocks:

- About me: 3-4 short lines with experience, teaching style, and who the tutor works with.
- Lesson formats: 1:1 lessons, conversation practice, business English, interview prep.
- Booking: available days or booking button.
- Pricing: simple price cards or "from" pricing if needed.
- Student results: 3 short testimonials.
- Materials: free checklist, mini-guide, placement test, or useful post.
- FAQ: level, cancellation, payment, first lesson, homework.
- Contact: Instagram, Telegram, email.

Useful buttons:

- Book a lesson.
- See lesson formats.
- Take placement test.
- Pay for a lesson.
- Join speaking club.
- Download free guide.

## Template B - Course Or Speaking Club Tutor

Hero:

- Name or course name.
- One-line outcome.
- Primary CTA: Join waitlist or book a call.
- Secondary CTA: See program.

Example positioning:

Business English speaking club for people who need confidence in meetings and interviews.

Core blocks:

- Next cohort date.
- Who it is for.
- What students practice.
- Weekly format.
- Price and payment options.
- Reviews from previous students.
- FAQ.
- Booking or application button.

## Personalization Checklist

Before creating a draft, capture:

- Exact niche: IELTS, IT English, business English, general adult English, Polish, German, French.
- Current bio link tool or lack of tool.
- Current CTA in bio.
- 2-3 offers visible from profile.
- Any repeated questions in comments.
- One content detail for the DM.
- Visual style: minimal, bright, academic, personal, premium, playful.
- Language: Ukrainian, English, Russian, mixed.

## Draft Copy Blocks

About block:

I help adults speak [language] with more confidence in [context]. My lessons are built around real situations: calls, interviews, work tasks, travel, presentations, or exams.

Lesson formats:

Choose the format that fits your goal: 1:1 lessons, speaking practice, exam prep, business communication, or a short consultation before you decide.

Booking block:

Pick a free slot and leave your contact. I will confirm the time and send the next steps.

Payment block:

Pay for a lesson, package, or speaking club in one place. If you are not sure which option fits, ask me before paying.

Testimonials block:

Add 3 short student reviews. Keep them concrete: result, timeline, and what changed.

FAQ block:

- What level do I need?
- How long is one lesson?
- Can I reschedule?
- Do you give homework?
- How do I pay?

## What To Avoid

- Too many equal buttons at the top.
- Generic "learn English easily" copy.
- Long biography before the booking CTA.
- Asking the tutor to fill everything alone.
- Sending the draft before the tutor has given permission or shown interest.
