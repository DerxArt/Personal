# Otake Tutor Growth Pilot

## Core bet

Перевірити вузький growth motion для викладачів іноземних мов дорослим: знайти тих, хто вже продає lessons, courses, speaking clubs або webinars через social content, а потім створювати персоналізований Otake draft **до** будь-якого прохання.

Головна гіпотеза: викладач, який регулярно відповідає в DM на однакові питання про ціну, формат, slots і booking, сильніше відреагує на готовий корисний draft, ніж на ще один generic tool pitch.

## Цільовий сегмент

**Primary:** tutors of foreign languages for adults.  
**Best first subset:** English tutors for adults із 1:1 lessons, group classes, speaking clubs, IELTS/FCE, business English або English for IT.

## Lead criteria

Пріоритет:

- активний content за останні 7–14 днів;
- чіткий adult-learning angle;
- sales signal: lesson, course, consultation, club, webinar, guide, waitlist;
- friction: «пишіть у direct», розкидані links, price in Highlights, generic Linktree, Telegram-only, weak booking path;
- достатня якість контенту, щоб custom page підсилювала credibility.

Уникати:

- неактивних профілів;
- відсутності monetization;
- суто children-focused без ясного parent journey;
- agencies зі зрілим funnel;
- профілів, де offer незрозумілий навіть після bio, highlights і pinned posts.

## Сегментація

| Audience size | Offer rule |
|---|---|
| Under 20K | Gift/help only; без ask на story, reel, recommendation або bio placement |
| 20K–50K | Gift/help first; light short bio test тільки після реального інтересу |
| 50K+ | Gift page/setup або paid route; rates лише після виявленого paid preference |

## Permission-based message flow

1. Конкретне human observation про profile/content.
2. Коротке питання: чи можна поділитися думкою.
3. Пояснення friction: repeated DMs, scattered formats, booking/payment confusion.
4. Offer: перший персоналізований draft + setup help, безкоштовно.
5. Draft link лише після permission/interest.
6. Ask про використання в bio лише після позитивної реакції.
7. Graceful exit без тиску.

Цей flow відображає сильний стиль Артура в sales communication: конкретика, персоналізація, низький тиск, value before ask.

## Tutor page blueprint

### Solo tutor

- hero: name, one-line positioning, photo;
- primary CTA: book a lesson;
- secondary CTA: ask a question;
- about;
- lesson formats;
- booking;
- pricing;
- testimonials;
- materials;
- FAQ;
- contact.

### Course / speaking club tutor

- next cohort date;
- who it is for;
- practice/outcome;
- weekly format;
- price/payment;
- reviews;
- FAQ;
- booking/application.

## 7-day test design

- Day 1: 50 leads, score fit/friction/content, top 20, two page structures.
- Days 2–3: 20 personalized drafts and first openers.
- Day 4: follow-up only with seen/interacted; adjust opener if reply rate <10%.
- Day 5: 2–3 interested leads review drafts; ask what is useful/wrong/missing.
- Day 6: check added links, clicks, edit requests; turn insights into organic content.
- Day 7: decide continue/change niche/narrow to business or IT English.

## Success signals

### Strong

- 50 leads researched;
- 20 drafts;
- 20 openers;
- 6+ replies;
- 3+ draft views/reviews;
- 2+ edit/setup requests;
- 1+ link in bio or shared with students.

### Good enough to iterate

- 3–5 replies;
- 1–2 reviews;
- clear objections fixable through copy or page structure.

### Weak

- <2 replies after 20 strong personalized openers;
- no draft views after permission;
- «useful, but not worth changing current flow».

## Зафіксовані операційні результати

### Apify lead scout, 01.07.2026

- 16 queries processed.
- 174 search candidates.
- 60 profiles checked in detail.
- 13 qualified leads.
- Segments: 8 Gift/help, 2 Gift/help first, 3 Gift/help or paid.

### First wave tracker, 02.07.2026

- 11 first-wave targets.
- 9 contacted.
- 1 DM blocked by Instagram error.
- 1 DM unavailable because profile exposed no message action.

### Broader Instagram inbox audit, 03.07.2026

- 259 chats classified.
- 144 needed follow-up now.
- 38 replies needed manual analysis.
- 14 were follow-up in 3 days; 14 in 2 days.
- 14 not interested; 13 not interested/unreachable.
- Other chats were distributed across 1/4/5-day follow-up and closure.

Raw names, messages and contact data were intentionally excluded from the portable vault.

## What this project proves

- Narrow-segment growth design.
- Lead scoring and qualification.
- Data-aware funnel thinking.
- Personalization at scale without generic spam.
- Permission-based sales communication.
- Clear test criteria and stop/iterate rules.
- Ability to connect product friction, page architecture and messaging.

## Main lessons

- A ready draft can communicate value better than a feature list.
- The first DM should earn permission, not force a link.
- Follower count changes the ask, not the human tone.
- «Useful» is not adoption; the real signal is edit request, setup help, bio usage or student action.
- Technical DM failure must be separated from message/segment failure.
- Cold outreach may be a useful experiment but is not a sustainable core role for Артур.
