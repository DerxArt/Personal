The working directory `C:\Users\derda\Threads` holds a 4-file content strategy system for the user's Threads account @d.artur.a, built 06.09.2026 from a full analysis of the account's own 10 posts (via Threads Insights) plus ~120 posts sampled across Ukrainian Threads (search-based, topics: Україна, біг, менеджмент, клієнт, нейромережі, стосунки) plus web research on Threads' algorithm/growth mechanics.

Files:
- `00-playbook.md` — **the one to open first**, every time a new thread/comment/content idea is requested. Contains voice checklist, 6 proven post-format templates, posting schedule, comment strategy, and 15 ready draft ideas.
- `01-profile-analysis.md` — full breakdown of all 10 of the account's own posts with view/like/comment metrics, what worked vs. flopped, tone-of-voice patterns observed.
- `02-ukrainian-threads-trends.md` — cross-niche patterns from ~120 sampled posts (question-hooks, client-dialogue format, "unpopular opinion" format, identity-check format, micro-stories, milestone posts) with real examples and engagement numbers.
- `03-threads-growth-guide.md` — general Threads algorithm mechanics (reply-weighted ranking, first-hour window, posting frequency/timing) sourced from web research, with sources listed.

**Why:** [[artur-derda-threads-creator]] asked for an ongoing system, not a one-off report — future thread/comment work should be judged against this playbook rather than generic advice.

**How to apply:** When asked to draft a thread, generate ideas, or write a comment for this account, read `00-playbook.md` first. Update the relevant file (not a new one) when new posts or new Insights data come in — the account posted in a single burst 25–28.07.2026 then went dark; if posting resumes, `01-profile-analysis.md`'s post table and conclusions should be refreshed with new data rather than left stale.
