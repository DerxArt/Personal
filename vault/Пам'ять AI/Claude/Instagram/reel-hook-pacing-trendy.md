Artur rejected a Reel-2 script draft (2026-09-11, `social/2026-09-11-reel-2-script.md` v1) as "занадто не цікавий, не звучить як трендовий рілс" even though it correctly followed the hook→context→shift→payoff structure and his voice punctuation rules. The specific problems: a generic meta-hook ("Тобі кажуть X, це брехня" framing announces the trick instead of dropping straight into it) and a "Перша спроба... Друга спроба..." labeled-list structure that reads like a report, not a spoken cold open.

**Why:** Structural correctness (hook/context/shift/payoff, real numbers, self-irony) is necessary but not sufficient — pacing and how the hook lands matter as much as the content. A meta-commentary hook that names the technique ("тобі кажуть... це брехня") is slower and more predictable than opening directly on a concrete, visual cold-open image. Explicit "Перша спроба / Друга спроба" labels feel like narration/reporting rather than someone talking.

**Fix applied, accepted by Artur:** Rewrote the hook to open on two concrete numbers/images with no meta-framing ("Два рази я вбивав звичку бігати. Один раз - за два кілометри. Другий - за один дощовий день.") instead of announcing a myth-busting move. Removed the "Перша спроба / Друга спроба" labels and let the two beats flow as continuous narration. Kept body-sensation detail ("темніє в очах, ноги ватні") over explanatory language. Ended on a punchy non-question mic-drop line ("Тепер правило одне: збився - пробіжка коротша, не довша пауза") rather than the generic audience-question CTA, consistent with [[reel-script-natural-delivery]]'s finding that his intro's non-question ending outperformed a question closer.

**How to apply:** When drafting a Reel script, before presenting it:
- Never open with a hook that names or announces the rhetorical move ("тобі кажуть X, це брехня", "ось міф про Y") — open directly on the concrete image/number/action instead, let the contrarian point land implicitly.
- Avoid labeled list structure ("Перша причина... Друга причина...", "Крок 1... Крок 2...") in spoken Reels even when the underlying content is a 2-3 item list — narrate it as continuous flow instead, per [[viral-reels-scripts-and-tz-architecture]]'s pattern-interrupt pacing guidance.
- Default to a non-question, ownable mic-drop ending unless there's a specific reason to solicit comments — matches his established pattern from the intro Reel.
- This is a pacing/delivery correction, not a contradiction of the structural formulas in [[viral-reels-scripts-and-tz-architecture]] or the voice rules in [[artur-derda-creator-voice]] — those stay in force; this adds "don't narrate the structure out loud."

**Exception confirmed 2026-09-11:** the "don't label list items out loud" rule applies to confession/narrative Reels, not to a straight Quick Tips format. When Artur explicitly asked for "3 причини + як виправити" (a direct advice-listicle, not a personal story), saying "перша / друга / третя" out loud while a big on-screen number counter appears is fine and expected — the numbered counter IS the pattern-interrupt in that format, so announcing the count reinforces it instead of slowing it down. Rule of thumb: narrate the count in a Quick Tips/advice Reel; don't narrate it in a confession/myth-reveal Reel.
