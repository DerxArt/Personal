Full breakdown saved at `research/ai-content-workflow-claude.md` in the Instagram project — a practical methodology (12 scenarios: strategy, profile packaging, idea generation, scriptwriting-in-own-style, covers, AI frames, carousels, Telegram/YouTube copywriting-scriptwriting-timecodes, presentations) for using Claude to speed up routine content work, sourced from a video by blogger Misha Timochka.

**Why:** Artur asked to analyze the video in detail and update the account's rules based on it (2026-09-11). Most of the video's own conclusion matches what's already established here: 100% of fully AI-automated accounts produce dead, templated content with near-zero views — AI drafts, human voice/judgment decides. This does NOT override [[artur-derda-creator-voice]] or [[reel-script-natural-delivery]]; those remain the mandatory filter any AI-drafted script/caption must pass before publishing.

**What's actually new/actionable for us right now:**
1. **"Customer journey" idea-generation method** — break the audience's path from zero to result into steps (e.g. beat fear → build a habit → survive day 3 → see progress → avoid burnout), then generate 10-15 concrete questions per step; each question = one Reel idea. Produces more concrete, useful ideas than generic "give me 100 ideas" because they're tied to a real moment in someone's journey, not an abstract topic.
2. **"Pains + obstacles" method** as a second idea generator — list 20 audience pains and 20 reasons they can't solve each pain (obstacles), then turn pain+obstacle pairs into Reel ideas that show how to overcome the obstacle.
3. Both methods are raw-idea generators only — manual selection of the 5-10 best, and passing any resulting draft through [[artur-derda-creator-voice]] / [[reel-script-natural-delivery]], stays mandatory and unchanged.

**Deliberately deprioritized / rejected for this account:**
- Higgsfield-style AI-generated "impossible to film" visuals, AI avatar/"soul" characters, and AI-polished glossy presentation aesthetics — these actively conflict with @artur.realist's core positioning "Не ідеальний - але росту" (real process, not a guru/AI success-theater aesthetic) per [[instagram-artur-realist-brand]].
- Telegram copywriter-cloning and YouTube strategy/scriptwriter/timecode scenarios from the video — not applicable, no active Telegram or YouTube channel for this account yet. Revisit if either channel launches.
- Auto-deriving a script-writing "skill" purely from transcribing 10-15 of Artur's own past Reels — skip for now since we don't have that many published Reels yet, and our style rules are already hand-documented and more rigorous (grep-verified punctuation bans) than what auto-extraction from transcripts would produce. Once 10-15 Reels are published, this method is worth running as a **cross-check** against the documented voice rules, not a replacement for them.

**How to apply:** When generating new Reel ideas for `content-plan/`, default to the customer-journey or pains+obstacles method from the playbook doc instead of generic brainstorming — it's more concrete and matches the audience-in-a-specific-moment framing already used in `research/self-improvement-niche-notes.md`. When Instagram Insights become available (see [[instagram-artur-realist-brand]] open constraint), consider the video's Claude Code pattern of dumping raw exported data into a file for Claude to analyze directly, rather than working from assumptions.
