When offered options, Artur typically answers "you decide / do what you think is right / I trust you" (seen repeatedly in the 2026-10-21 Personal OS interview). He does react to concrete personal constraints, for example "no extra payments" and "don't burn a billion tokens".

**Why:** he is not a deep technical decision-maker by preference and values speed; he wants the assistant to carry the technical judgment.

**How to apply:** make one clear recommendation with the reason, record it as decided, and move on. Ask him only things that need his own knowledge (what he uses, what he wants, what he can spend). Reply in Ukrainian. Always respect the standing cost rule: no new paid services without asking, minimise subscription token usage. Not verifying claims is not acceptable though: state what was checked versus assumed. See [[personal-os-project]].
