Do not write Instagram Reel scripts as a string of polished, quotable one-liners back to back — even though that density works in his written voice ([[artur-derda-creator-voice]]), on camera it reads as scripted and undercuts the "не ідеальний" (not perfect) premise the whole account is built on. Artur rejected a first-draft Reel script rewrite specifically because it "виглядає взагалі не природно" (looks completely unnatural) even though it correctly followed his written-voice rules (hook-first, concrete numbers, self-deprecating line, question CTA).

**Why:** A LinkedIn post is read silently, so back-to-back aphorisms feel sharp on the page. Spoken aloud to camera, the same density sounds memorized/performed — real people don't produce 5-6 quotable lines in a row unscripted. Hook/retention engineering (open loops, pattern interrupts, algorithm-weighted CTAs) doesn't fix this; it's a performance/authenticity problem, not a distribution problem, and stacking more hook theory on top of an over-written script makes it worse, not better.

**Default to light-touch, not rewriting.** Across this whole thread, Artur's own instincts beat every proposed change: his original hook beat two alternatives, and his own full rewrite (specific numbers - snoozed 4 times, woke at 11am; the "пішов читати" excuse; the game reframed as "персонаж першого рівня" instead of a rules explanation; the non-question mic-drop ending "прокачка мого персонажа оголошується відкритою") was better than the talking-points version this file was originally built around. Going forward: offer structure/talking-points only when he's starting from nothing or explicitly asks for a rewrite. When he brings his own draft, do light technical polish (timing, on-screen text cues, punctuation per [[artur-derda-creator-voice]]) rather than restructuring it, and don't push generic rules (e.g. "CTA must be a question") against something that's already working and ownable - offer trade-offs as optional notes, never as fixes to make.

**How to apply:** For Reel scripts going forward:
- Cut to ONE strong/ownable line per Reel, not several — let the rest be loose, plain, connective tissue
- Default to a loose talking-points outline ("say this in your own words, don't memorize") rather than a word-for-word script — improvised connective tissue is what actually sounds natural
- It's fine (good, even) to include an example "take" with natural speech texture — false starts, trailing off, casual fillers ("ну", "типу", "от") — as long as it's labeled as one possible take, not a script to recite
- Offer show-don't-tell / minimal-narration as an alternative when a topic allows it (real footage of the moment + on-screen text, little or no spoken monologue) — especially useful before he has more camera reps
- Read any draft aloud once before presenting it; if it sounds like writing being performed, cut lines until it doesn't
- When Artur pushes back on a hook rewrite, restore his original wording rather than proposing yet another replacement — he explicitly preferred his own hook ("Я зараз скажу річ, після якої частина людей від мене відпишеться") over two different alternative hooks offered instead. His instinct for what he's comfortable saying outweighs generic hook-optimization theory.
- Reuse concrete details he's already given as the story anchor instead of asking for a brand-new specific instance — e.g. "двадцять хвилин вибирав музику для тренування, а тоді вирішив що сьогодні був день підготовки" from his own first draft was the real anchor for the XP-system story beat, no invented example needed.
