Artur's written voice (LinkedIn, blog, Instagram captions/carousels) is documented precisely in `strategy/tone-of-voice-linkedin.md` (provided 2026-09-08, based on a grep-verified analysis of 15+ real posts over ~6 months). This supersedes any looser earlier summary where they differ — the punctuation rules especially are non-negotiable and were previously understated here.

**Structure formula (almost always this order):**
1. Hook-statement, 1-2 sentences, often paradoxical/counterintuitive ("Найнебезпечніший стейкхолдер - не той, хто проти тебе. А той, хто занадто «за».")
2. Personal story/concrete case with real detail (place, time, people's actual words, numbers). Never starts with abstract theory.
3. The turn/realization ("І тут почалось найцікавіше...", "А насправді проблема була в...")
4. Generalization-conclusion, often a short aphorism
5. A question to the audience, almost always ("А у вас було таке...?", "Ви до якого табору?")

**Sentence style:** short, chopped sentences, often no conjunction at the start ("Не працює...", "Секунда тиші.", "Ноль."). One paragraph = one thought, heavy use of line breaks (mobile-scannable). Colloquial, not corporate — real/vivid/slang words where they fit ("дурак", "бовкнув", "накричав", "крінж"). Self-irony and vulnerability, but not overdone/performed ("без награності"). Numbers and specifics always ("15 застосунків", "9 місяців", "$400K", "2-3 рази на тиждень"). Rhythmic anaphora works well: "Записати задачу - легко. Відкрити список наступного дня - складніше. Виконувати все системно - окремий жанр фантастики."

**Signature device — everyday metaphor mapped onto a bigger insight:** checkout counter → project management, running → productivity, a Tinder date → networking. Take a mundane, unprofessional situation and pull a real insight out of it. Directly reusable for Instagram running/discipline content since it's already his established move.

**Recurring themes:** "I'm not an expert, I'm learning as I go" (main throughline — failure/uncertainty/learning-through-practice, not ready-made advice); real operational detail from Otake (Jira, sprints, backlog, calls with the CEO) for insider credibility; mental health/rest/guilt-about-doing-nothing as a recurring, not one-off, topic; career uncertainty framed as content, not a weakness to hide.

**What he never does:** motivational banalities without a concrete tie-in; artificial "5 lessons I learned" emoji-bullet lists (even when list-like structure exists, it's woven into the narrative, not bulleted); showing off a result without its failure-context (even a product-launch post is framed as "honest, still rough, give feedback," not "look at my win"); clickbait headlines like "you won't believe" — hooks are provocative but substantive.

**Punctuation — strict, grep-verified, treat as non-negotiable:**
- **Em dash "—": NEVER use it.** Effectively zero occurrences across 15+ real posts (1 stray case total). Explicitly flagged as *the* top technical tell of AI-generated text, in Ukrainian and English both. If the instinct is to reach for "—", use a period (new sentence) instead, or a simple hyphen.
- **Simple hyphen with spaces " - "**: this is his actual pause/contrast device (70 occurrences) — use it freely for pauses, definitions, contrasts: "На одному проєкті була дівчина - не власниця, не спонсор, звичайний стейкхолдер."
- **Straight quotes "..."**: almost never (3 occurrences total) — don't wrap concept-phrases in quotes "just in case."
- **Ukrainian guillemets «...»**: rare and pointed (~20 occurrences), always around a single word or short term, max 1-2 times per whole post. Never around a full sentence or an imagined line of dialogue.
- **Pre-publish self-test:** if a draft has even one em dash, or more than one pair of guillemets, or any straight quotes around a concept-phrase — that's almost certainly an AI-slop tell. Remove it before showing him.

**Why:** This voice is validated against his real published output, not inferred — and the punctuation rules in particular are invisible unless checked mechanically, which is exactly why they were undercooked in earlier drafts (an em dash slipped into a reel-script caption before this document existed). [[instagram-artur-realist-brand]] and [[reel-script-natural-delivery]] both still apply: this is his WRITTEN voice (captions, carousels, LinkedIn/blog). Reel scripts that are spoken aloud still need the looser, talking-points treatment described in [[reel-script-natural-delivery]] — this document makes the written register more precise, it doesn't change that a script read aloud is a different register.

**How to apply:** Use this for carousel slides, captions, and any LinkedIn/blog content. Before presenting a written draft, mechanically check it against the punctuation test above. Ask for the concrete story/numbers behind a topic before drafting — never invent an event, a number, or a specific instance.
